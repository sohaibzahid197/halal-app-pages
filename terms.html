Guidance, not a fatwa

Verdicts are guidance produced by the app’s published rules and your settings. They are not a fatwa, a religious ruling or a halal certification. When in doubt, ask a scholar you trust and check the product’s certification.

Product data can be wrong

Ingredient lists come from Open Food Facts contributors or from your own photo. Recipes change and labels can be incomplete. Always read the label on the pack in your hand.
