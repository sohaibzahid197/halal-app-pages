Data sources and licences

Last updated 12 October 2026

Open Food Facts

Product names, ingredient lists, labels and photos come from Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL). Product photos are under Creative Commons BY-SA.

USDA FoodData Central

When Open Food Facts has no ingredient list, the app may use the US Department of Agriculture's FoodData Central branded foods data, which is in the public domain (CC0). It mainly covers products sold in the United States.

Halal rules

Ingredient rules are written by the HalalLenz team from published fatwas, halal standards and certifier guidance, with references shown on each ingredient. They follow published sources, are versioned in the app, and have not been reviewed individually by a scholar. Each verdict shows which rule decided it.

Certification marks

Certification body names are used to recognise labels only. HalalLenz is not affiliated with any certification body.

Ramadan dates

Ramadan start dates are estimates from the Umm al-Qura calendar and can differ by a day where you live.

Fonts and icons

Fraunces by Undercase Type under the SIL Open Font Licence. Icons drawn for HalalLenz.
