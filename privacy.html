Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Summary

HalalLens has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your profile, history and lists stay on this device.

Barcodes

When you scan or type a barcode, only that number is sent to Open Food Facts, a public non-profit database, to look up the product. Open Food Facts receives your IP address like any website. No photo, name or profile is sent. Product photos are loaded from Open Food Facts’ image servers. When you report a mistake or ask a manufacturer, your own Mail app sends the email.

Search

When you search for a product, the words you type are sent to Open Food Facts to find matching products.

Label photos

Photos of ingredient lists are read on this device with Apple’s text recognition. The photo is deleted as soon as the text is read and is never uploaded. The text is kept with your history so you can see the result again.

Sharing with Open Food Facts (off by default)

If you sign in with your own Open Food Facts account and turn sharing on, the barcode and the ingredient text you confirmed are sent to Open Food Facts and become public under its ODbL licence. Photos are never sent. Your username and password stay in this device's secure storage.

Camera and notifications

The camera is only used while the scanner is open. Notifications are only used for the Ramadan reminders you turn on, and are scheduled on this device.

Your data

You can delete everything at any time in Settings → Erase all my data. Deleting the app also deletes all of its data.
